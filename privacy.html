Privacy Policy

Clipwave (com.clipwave.videomaker) · Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Clipwave ("the app") uses, why, and who it goes to. Clipwave has no user accounts: we do not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number.

Your photos and videos

Photos you pick are read on your phone to make your video. Videos are made and kept on your phone; if you tap Save, the video is stored in your phone's Movies/Clipwave folder. Deleting a video, using "Delete My Data" in Settings, or uninstalling the app removes the copies the app keeps.

For templates made for people, the app checks on your phone that the photo shows a face (Google ML Kit face detection runs on the device; the photo is not uploaded for this).

AI features

Some features use artificial intelligence on a server:

  • Prompt help and video planning. When you use Surprise me, Enrich or make a video from text, the text you typed is sent to our AI server (llm.braincuber.com) to write or improve the prompt and to plan the video.
  • Photo check. Only if you allow AI features when asked, a small copy of your photo (about 512 pixels) is sent to the same AI server to check that it is suitable for the template. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Pictures for text videos. To draw a picture that matches your words, a picture description (not your photo) is sent to Pollinations (image.pollinations.ai), an AI image service, which returns a picture.
  • AI video on our server. If server video making is switched on in a later version, your photo and prompt are sent to our video server and its AI video provider to make the clip, and the finished video is downloaded to your phone. The app asks for your permission before a photo leaves your phone.

Our AI server processes this data to answer your request and does not use it to identify you. Do not use photos of other people without their permission.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may fill them through partner networks (Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Liftoff (Vungle), Pangle, InMobi and Mintegral). To show and measure ads, these companies may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and how ads are viewed and tapped. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices in the app. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. Google's use of data: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

App settings and catalogue downloads

When it starts, the app downloads its settings (which ads are on, how often they may appear, coin prices, update notices) from our settings server. The request contains the app's package name and version, the settings version the app already has and a random install ID made by the app (not linked to you, your account or your advertising ID). The app also downloads its list of templates and their pictures from GitHub Pages and the picture links in that list. Like any web request, these servers see your IP address.

Statistics and messages

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app and its ads perform (screens used, ad requests, ads shown, taps and ad revenue), with an app instance ID, device model, Android version, country and app version. Your photos, prompts and videos are never included.

The app can receive announcements through Firebase Cloud Messaging. Firebase gives your installation a random token and the app joins general groups based on language, country and app version. The app also shows a notification when your video is ready. You can turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Pro and coin packs are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app keeps your coin balance and Pro status on your phone.

Updates and reviews

On phones with Google Play, the app asks Google Play whether a newer version is available and may show Google Play's rating window if you choose to rate the app.

Data sale and sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. Data reaches the companies named above only for the purposes described here.

Children

Clipwave is not directed at children under 13, and ads are limited to family-friendly (PG) content.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and noted in an app update. Questions: use the developer contact on Clipwave's Google Play page.
